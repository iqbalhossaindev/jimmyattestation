System Notes

This project includes full attendance flow, GPS geofence validation, face image capture, sales checkout, admin product management, and monthly PDF report generation. Face verification uses free manual selfie review: merchandisers submit a store-background selfie at check-in and check-out, then admin approves or rejects it from the backend. Selfie photos are deleted automatically after 40 days.
